## Choose external devices

To make a Raspberry Pi work as a full computer you need four things:

**Power** — the connector and power supply depend on the model.

**A microSD card** — the software lives here.

**A screen or display** — this plugs into the HDMI socket.

**A keyboard (and optional mouse)** — this plugs into a USB socket.
